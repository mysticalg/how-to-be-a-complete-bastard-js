About this recovery milestone

This is an incomplete, native JavaScript reconstruction from the supplied alternate snapshot. The renderer has passed byte comparisons against the original for all 14 room layouts and four camera angles. Navigation, search, item menus and action tables are being recovered. Guest AI, effects, scoring, endings and sound are still under verification. It does not run Spectrum instructions.

Walk into furniture and press Action to search it. With no target, Action opens your pockets. Q/A or arrows select menu entries; Space/Enter chooses.
